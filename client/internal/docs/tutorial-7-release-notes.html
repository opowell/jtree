Tutorial: Release Notes

Release Notes

PLANNED
  • Generic game trees.
  • Listen to incremental game changes via observer.

Unreleased

  • The admins' menus and panels are appfr 0.68.0: the search box over the admin's lists has an edge and a field's fill, and the number beside each type in a list is abbreviated past a thousand (1.2k).
  • The admin's panels sit straight in the window, without the bar and border the outermost space drew around them ("Row"); how that space is shown (a row, a column, tabs or a desktop) is chosen from Window → Arrange. The admins' menus and panels are appfr 0.58.0.
  • The admin: a pane of tabs' ⋯ menu has Close all, which closes every tab on it that can be closed. The admins' menus and panels are appfr 0.57.0.
  • The admin's lists sort by any column: press its heading (an app's file, kind, folder or status, a participant's app, period, group or stage, a log entry's event, ...). An app's Kind says what it is: jtree app, oTree app, z-Tree treatment or questionnaire, queue, or oTree session config (they were all "app" or "queue"); an oTree app's file is its folder (it was its __init__.py) and a session config's is settings.py › <name> (it was settings.py#<name>). Apps and queues can stay out of the admin's lists of apps: app.listed = false in a jtree app, queue.listed = false (or "listed": false) in a queue, JTREE_LISTED = False in an oTree app's C, jtree_listed=False in a session config; Browse's "Unlisted" filter shows them.
  • A new session opens in the admin's Session panel, in a window of its own filling the page, whether it is started from an app's panel, the App editor, File → New session, the Session panel or Browse. What was open before stays as it was, as another window.
  • The admin's Window menu lists the windows open ("Browse + trust", "Session", say: a window is named for the panels in it) and brings the one chosen to the front; Arrange shows them as a row, a column, tabs or a desktop. Browse, Session, Participants, Participant views, Log and Settings are opened and closed from a new Panels menu (they were in the Window menu). With several windows, Arrange also has Next tab, Previous tab and Close all. The admins' menus and panels are appfr 0.59.0.
  • The admin tells apps of the same name apart: where several share one (cournot as a jtree app, an oTree app and a z-Tree treatment, say), its app lists and menus put the folders that make each unique in front (jtree/cournot, otree/cournot, ztree/cournot).
  • GIMS (client/apps/gims) is made of apps of their own, each with the presets of the studies it is part of (under the names of their markets), and an example to play it alone: the market (market/, with the engine), the control questions, the cognitive reflection test and Holt and Laury's lottery choices before or after it, the chat, bonds and forecasts before trading, the bonds back, the forecasts paid and a lottery ticket after it, the account status and a rating after a period's results, and the payment. gims/app.jtt is a wrapper: for a study, it adds the apps its presets turn on as its stages, with the stage ids GIMS's had (controlQuestions, crtBefore, riskAfter, payment, ...); the market adds the apps of each period as its own stages (its periodApps option), played by its traders on their accounts. A study plays as before, its settings and its "changes" the same; the bonds back, the forecasts' earnings, a lottery ticket's draw, the account status and the rating have screens of their own, and the lottery choices' result screen can be left out (holtLauryResult, off for Breaban and Noussair, whose lotteries come before the market). Played alone, the market pays its rounds as points, and the payment app pays the session's apps before it.
  • oTree apps: a page's js_vars are computed again when the page connects (or is reloaded), as oTree computes them for each request: a page that loads after its stage started gets them as they are then (the time since an auction started, say). The samples' centipede and real-effort jtree apps can now be described as app descriptions (their functions need nothing from the file but app, and the work stage's length is read from its option when it starts).
  • GIMS asset markets (client/apps/gims): the Graz-Innsbruck Market System's generic asset market (Palan 2015) as a jtree app. One or more assets traded in a continuous double auction, a call auction or both, with GIMS's order rules (validation and invalidation of orders, the improvement and no-crossing rules, fees, shorting, its five-step call auction price) and settings (endowment types, dividends, buyback values, practice periods, the vote to end a period, control questions, the CRT, Holt and Laury's lotteries), and more for published designs: repeated markets, a dividend account apart from cash, interest on cash, cash inflows, holding caps, purchase limits, buyer and seller roles, and price forecasts paid by accuracy. 23 presets, chosen when a session starts: GIMS's defaults and 22 published designs, from Smith, Suchanek and Williams (1988) to Tucker and Xu (2024), each with its source; a "changes" option changes any setting. See the new page, "GIMS asset markets". Then 59 more, for 25 more studies of the asset market experiments list (Haruvy and Noussair 2006 to Shestakova, Powell and Gladyrev 2019), with what their designs need: holding taxes, drawn final values with supplements, insiders, private signals or public news, value paths, a cash reserve for short positions, a book of depth one, announcements, a chat, bonds and an interest rate policy, a computerized firm, lottery tickets, an account status and rating after each period, period lengths by round, cash carried between markets, earnings as profits, one market paid from each block, subsidies, and a maximum payment.
  • The askBeforeLeaving setting (default true): false stops participants' pages asking before they are closed or reloaded. Changing the admin password or "multiple users" in the admin keeps the other settings in settings.json (it rewrote the file with those two alone).
  • A samples library (client/apps/samples): 23 economics experiments, from dictator and trust games to auctions and an asset market, each as a jtree app, an oTree app (in an oTree project, a session config each) and a z-Tree treatment, with the same design and payoffs. It gathers jtree's former sample games (1 sample games, now in it), oTree's sample games and z-Tree's examples. See the new page, "Samples library". The public goods game's screens say "points" (they said "E$"), as the tutorials' now do.
  • The App editor and oTree Studio keep a file's line ends: an app with Windows' (\r\n) gets them in what an edit puts in too (edits mixed them).
  • oTree apps: the bots' call_live_method (tests.py) runs, once per group and live page, as in oTree; an app in a project runs with the project's settings (POINTS_DECIMAL_PLACES, USE_POINTS, ...) even when started alone; ExtraModel.create gives the fields it is not given their initial value; and an amount equals a number as oTree has it (cu(38) == 37.5 with whole points).
  • One model for apps, stages and queues: an app is a list of stages, and a stage is an app (stage instanceof App). A stage can have stages of its own (stage.newStage(id)), played stage.numPeriods times each time a group reaches it, in the group or regrouped (groupSize, or another matching, on the stage), with an app's hooks, and a duration for the group's time in them all. app.addApp(file, options) makes another app's file a stage, loaded afresh each time with its own periods, groups and page, bringing the stage settings its file sets (waitToEnd, groupStart, ...). A queue is an app whose stages are its apps: app.addApp('my-queue.jtq') adds it as a stage (added to a session, its apps are the session's, as before). Their data follows their app's in the session's file, and a saved session loads them where they were. App descriptions (IR) have them too. Apps written as before run as before. See the new tutorial, "Apps and stages".
  • A player is saved after playerEnd: what it sets in a period's last stage is in a saved session too (it was lost when the session was loaded).
  • The app editors' code fields are a code editor (CodeMirror 6) instead of plain text boxes: syntax colours for JavaScript, HTML, CSS and Python, line numbers, Tab to indent, matching brackets, search (Ctrl+F) and completion (Ctrl+Space), in jtree's App editor (functions, screens, a script's code, an element's HTML, Source), the z-Tree admin's program and screen dialogs, and oTree Studio (functions, methods, templates, __init__.py, settings.py). They follow the admin's light or dark theme.
  • The admin's open panels and their arrangement are in its address (?w=…): reloading the page keeps them, and a link opens them as they were. A panel can be popped out to a browser window of its own (its ⋯ menu). Moving or closing every panel on one side of the default layout no longer leaves an empty box there. The z-Tree admin's windows are in its address too. The admins' menus and panels are appfr 0.56.0.
  • The admin's text is larger: 15px body text (it was 13px), 14px for the App editor's tree, labels and buttons, 13.5px for code, in every theme.
  • The admin's inherit and mono-size themes are readable in dark mode: the page gives them light text on its dark background, and a sans-serif font (they had black text and the browser's serif font).
  • The App editor: drag and drop in its tree (elements before, after or into others, onto another screen or stage, copied with Ctrl or Alt or into another app's editor; stages into another order), and a history of named changes: Undo and Redo name what they undo and redo and choose again what was chosen then, and the History list goes to any step.
  • The App editor's Structured mode: an app as a tree of its stages, their screens and the elements on them (paragraphs, inputs, buttons, ...), and their functions, as z-Tree and oTree Studio show theirs. Elements are added from a palette, changed in fields (an input's variable, type, limits, ...), moved, copied, pasted and deleted, each change made to that element alone in the source. Forms and Source are the editor's other modes; Undo and Redo work in all three. A stage renamed in the editor takes a new variable name too, if the editor named its variable.
  • App editors: jtree's own in the admin (File → New app…, or an app's Edit): an app's settings, stages, screens and functions, and its whole source. The z-Tree admin edits z-Tree treatments as z-Tree does (Treatment → New …, element dialogs, undo, Check) and saves them in z-Tree's text format. The oTree admin's Studio edits oTree projects and apps as oTree Studio does. Each changes only the part of the file it is about.
  • z-Tree treatments run in jtree: .ztt files from z-Tree 3 to 5, z-Tree's text export and questionnaires (.ztq), their programs in z-Tree's language, and z-Leaf's screens in participants' browsers, plot boxes, contract lists and table loaders and dumpers included. Checked against z-Tree 5.1.21.
  • oTree apps run in jtree, in oTree's current format and its older one (models.py, Django templates), with their Python (in Pyodide, nothing to install), templates, live pages, chat, timeouts, ExtraModel, admin reports and bots. A project's session configs are queues and its ROOMS rooms. Checked against oTree 5.11.5, 3.4.0 and 2.5.8: the same data recorded.
  • An admin for oTree users at /admin/otree/: sessions from session configs, links, monitor, data, reports, payments, rooms.
  • Converters: z-Tree treatments (Treatment → Convert to jtree… in the z-Tree admin) and oTree apps (Convert to jtree in the oTree admin) to jtree apps, with a report of anything left to do by hand.
  • Play with bots: a session of oTree apps is played by their bots, or a generic bot; z-Tree treatments by a generic z-Tree bot.
  • Modes: whose rules apply where z-Tree, oTree and jtree differ (the mode setting, --mode=, JTREE_MODE, or a session's "Rules").
  • Downloads: a session's data as oTree's CSVs (all apps wide, per app, page times, custom_export) too.
  • Rooms: a session can be opened in a room, and the room's participants go to their page in it.
  • participant.skipToApp(index) ends a participant's app and starts a later one.
  • Payment: sessions' showUpFee, exchangeRate and currency (defaults in settings.json), app.exchangeRate, and participant.payment().
  • The server checks forms: app.fields declares fields' types and limits, stage.formFields what a form must send, and stage.validate(player, values) checks them together. A form with mistakes is not stored and the page shows why.
  • A player's own time for a stage (stage.clientDuration, or stage.getClientDuration(player)) is kept by the server too: a page that does not submit when the time is up is ended after timeoutGrace. stage.endOnTimeout = false keeps a timed-out stage open until the players submit.
  • period.setGroups(matrix) in app.periodStart regroups a period; app.groupByArrival groups participants in the order they arrive.
  • player.inPeriod(n), player.inAllPeriods(), player.inPreviousPeriods(), and the same for groups. group.old() works (it threw).
  • The PARTNER_1122 and PARTNER_1212 matchings work (they threw when a period started).
  • stage.waitForAllGroups: no group starts the stage until every group of the period has arrived; then stage.allGroupsStart(period) runs, once.
  • App hooks app.appStart(), app.periodStart(period) and app.periodEnd(period); app.end() is called again when everyone has finished an app, and the app's data is written to the session's CSV file then, as before.
  • When a group ends a stage that waits for everyone, players move on after groupEnd, not before it: the next stage, or the next period's first, no longer starts before groupEnd has run. Errors in groupEnd and playerEnd are logged instead of stopping the session.
  • Apps as folders work: app.jtt with one .jtt file per stage beside it, added in name order (1_decide.jtt is stage decide). In a stage's file, stage is the stage and app the app. A folder's stage files no longer show up as apps of their own.
  • Stage timeouts no longer depend on participants' pages: if a page does not submit within stage.timeoutGrace seconds (default 5) of the timeout, the server ends the stage for that player. player.timedOut tells playerEnd and groupEnd which players timed out.
  • A syntax error in an app is reported with its line and position, also in a folder app's stage files (app.errorFile).
  • jt.stop() stops a jtree started from another program, for example tests.
  • Tests: cd server && pnpm test.
  • The moving-slider feature app runs on its own.
  • Saved sessions load again (the loadSessions setting), and carry on where they stopped: participants reconnect to their stage, groups' tables (e.g. a double auction's offers) are back, and a stage timer runs on with the time it had left when jtree stopped (paused, if the session was). Since mid-2019, session files separated each record's type with the CSV delimiter (;), which is not JSON, so nothing was loaded; such files are read too. Apps are loaded from the session's own copy, folder apps included.
  • Participants removed from a session stay removed when it is loaded; a session that had started is not started again.
  • jtree saves the time every 5 seconds (autoSaveFreq, previously 100), and when it stops: after a crash, restored stage timers are off by at most that much.

2026.10.05 - 0.9.0

  • Downloads bundle Node.js (current LTS) and run through JAS: unpack and run start.command (macOS), start.cmd (Windows) or start.sh (Linux). Native Apple Silicon build. Windows XP and 32-bit Windows are no longer supported.
  • Admin access: without an admin password, the admin interface only opens on the computer running jtree. Set defaultAdminPwd in settings.json to log in from other computers. Previously any participant could connect as an admin.
  • Session output downloads and /api need admin access.
  • New default admin interface at /admin (admin2), which runs without a build step. The previous one is at /admin/multiuser. The Vue 2 "beta" admin is removed.
  • Admin interface laid out after z-Tree at /admin/ztree, also without a build step.
  • Server: socket.io 4, Express 5, Node.js 22 or later. Unused dependencies removed.
  • Participant page: jQuery 3.7.1 (with jQuery Migrate, for code written for jQuery 1.x) and Vue 2.7.16. The old files stay in /shared for apps that load them.
  • Downloads serve jtree from the root (/admin/, /P1). Added to a JAS that hosts other apps, jtree is served under /jtree.
  • Starts on port 3000, or the next free port. The PORT environment variable sets where to start.
